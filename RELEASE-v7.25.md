# VETROMIR 7.25: Fiercer Encounters

Free for Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 13+. English and Serbian in
one app, with shared saved progress. Published September 12, 2026.

## What's Changed

- **918 powerful ordinary enemies** across ten chapters, with all 16 species
  and relic guardians retained. This is +30% over the local 7.24 build,
  rounded up per chapter. Compared with public 7.21, encounters are fewer
  but individual enemies are much tougher and the roster has refreshed artwork.
- **Boss health +25%**: 525 to 3,075 base HP across the ten guardians.
  **Nedah: 15,751 HP and five phases**, the sum of the first ten guardians.
- Bosses close distance faster, recover sooner between combos and choose
  attacks based on player distance, height and movement. Warnings remain
  readable, with a minimum recovery window after a complete combo.
- **Three core powers per hero**: ranged, melee and a unique special.
  The fourth slot is reserved for attacks found during the run. O/R3 and
  the former SUPER button are aliases for the same special.
- New painted enemy silhouettes, clearer ranged weapon paths, returning
  blades and attack-link bonuses from the previously local 7.22-7.24 updates.
- First loot after the second ordinary enemy defeat, then every fourth.
  Four types of limited-use attacks; summoned minions cannot farm these drops.
- Boss afterimages and initial minions are prepared during loading. Hidden
  fallback models are paused, unchanged HUD colours are cached, and impact
  animations no longer pile up. Full HD resolution and visible animation remain.
- Additional regression checks for population, boss health, attack recovery,
  empty particle bursts, HUD heart bounds and English boss warnings.

## Download And Install

Choose the **DMG**, open it and drag **Vetromir.app** into **Applications**.
The ZIP contains the same bilingual app. Replace the previous app; your local
progress is kept. The automatic GitHub "Source code" download is not an installer.

This build is ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. macOS may block the first
launch. After trying to open the app, use **System Settings > Privacy & Security
> Open Anyway** only if you trust this official download. Do not disable
system-wide security. [Installation help](https://github.com/srdjankotarlic/vetromir-sivi-source/blob/main/INSTALL-EN.md).

## Tested Scope

Build, profile, campaign, all eight heroes, movement, combat, arsenal, all eleven
bosses, enemy tactics, obstacles, audio and both languages are covered by the
automated release gates. Full HD rendering is tested on an Apple M1 Pro.
See [QA-v7.25.md](https://github.com/srdjankotarlic/vetromir-sivi-source/blob/main/QA-v7.25.md) for measurements and limitations.

This is a Mac release, not a new iPhone/iPad, Intel Mac, Windows or Android build.
No new trailer is included. Current images are captured from this version.
