# VETROMIR 7.25: Fiercer Encounters

## Facts

- Developer: Srđan Kotarlić, independent creator.
- Platform: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 13+.
- Price: free. No account, ads, subscriptions or in-game purchases.
- Languages: English and Serbian in one app.
- Eight playable heroes, three core powers per hero, four discoverable attacks.
- Ten chapters, 918 ordinary enemies across 16 species, eleven bosses.
- Solo or two-player local co-op. Remappable keyboard/mouse/controller input.
- Original AI-assisted painted cartoon artwork with articulated animation.
- Ad-hoc signed; not Apple-notarized. See installation instructions.
