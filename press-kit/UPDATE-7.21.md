# VETROMIR 7.21: Curious Arsenal

Release: September 10, 2026. Mac desktop, English and Serbian, free.
Creator: Srđan Kotarlić. Native Swift/SpriteKit. Solo or two-player local co-op.

## Short Description

Bring the wind back to Mrakovina in a colourful, native Mac action-platformer.
Choose from eight heroes with unusual weapons, find limited-use attacks during
the journey, and beat ten guardians to unlock the final battle in the Zero Sky.

## What Is New

Orbiting rings, returning shears, floating anchors, remote mines, a light parasol,
cutting thread, seed sentinels and a lantern that remembers impacts. Every hero
has a secondary combination that fits their primary weapon. Enemy drops add
comets, a clock marble, a mirror box and a bouncing thunder die to the fourth slot.
