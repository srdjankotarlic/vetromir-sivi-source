# VETROMIR 7.21: Curious Arsenal

Free Mac download. English and Serbian in one application.

## New In This Release

- Eight primary weapon systems and eight distinct starting secondary attacks.
- Mines and remote detonations, stitch-and-wire traps, anchor combinations,
  orbiting rings, returning shears, reflected shots, seed sentinels and memory echoes.
- Four attacks to collect from defeated enemies, with limited charges and a
  temporary fourth-slot override that preserves your equipment.
- Clearer scissor anticipation, platform-aware mine collisions and pause-safe traps.
- The cartoon world, articulated heroes, smaller connected heads, blinking,
  cloth motion and movement refinements from the recent desktop updates.
- Ten chapters, eleven bosses, local co-op and the full ending remain included.

## Download And Install

Choose **Vetromir-7.21-macOS-Apple-Silicon-Serbian-English.dmg**, open it, and
drag Vetromir into Applications. The ZIP is an alternative containing the same app.
Requires **Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or newer**.

The app is ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. After trying to launch, macOS
may require **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**. Do not disable
Gatekeeper globally. [Installation instructions](https://github.com/srdjankotarlic/vetromir-sivi-source/blob/main/INSTALL-EN.md).

Both languages share the same profile. Updating does not reset saved progress.
This is a Mac release; the older iPhone package has not been updated.

## Verification

The packaged app passed the game, movement, arsenal, boss, campaign, audio and
language regression checks. Tests cover all eight heroes at 30/60/120 simulation
steps per second. See [tested scope and limitations](https://github.com/srdjankotarlic/vetromir-sivi-source/blob/main/QA-v7.21.md).
